About Me

Trustworthy AI, graph intelligence, and cybersecurity for real-world impact.

I am a computer scientist and cybersecurity faculty member with a Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of North Texas. My research spans trustworthy artificial intelligence, AI security, adversarial machine learning, graph neural networks, representation learning, predictive modeling, large-scale graph analytics, and high-performance computing.

I am particularly interested in developing AI systems that remain robust, secure, interpretable, and reliable under realistic and adversarial conditions.

My interdisciplinary research has included cybersecurity, socioeconomic prediction, public-health modeling, biomedical AI, dynamic networks, and responsible AI.

Research Areas

Secure, trustworthy, and graph-based artificial intelligence.

Trustworthy AI & AI Security

Studying the security, reliability, robustness, and responsible deployment of AI systems in adversarial and high-impact environments.

Adversarial Machine Learning

Evaluating feature and structural attacks, model resilience, attack mitigation, and defense strategies for machine-learning and graph-learning systems.

Graph Neural Networks

Designing GCN, GraphSAGE, graph-augmented, and representation-learning approaches for structured data, prediction, and community discovery.

AI for Cybersecurity

Applying artificial intelligence and machine learning to phishing detection, cyber defense, anomalous behavior analysis, secure systems, and emerging cyber threats.

Large-Scale Graph Analytics & HPC

Developing scalable graph algorithms, including k-core and k-truss methods, for large dynamic networks using high-performance and parallel computing.

AI for Health & Society

Applying graph machine learning and predictive modeling to public health, socioeconomic data, biomedical applications, and geospatial decision support.

Featured Research Projects

Selected work across trustworthy AI, cybersecurity, graph learning, public health, and scalable computing.

Adversarial Robustness of Graph AI

Evaluating GCN, GraphSAGE, and hybrid graph-machine-learning models under feature, structural, and mixed adversarial perturbations.

Similarity-Consistency Filter (SCF)

A graph-defense strategy designed to identify and suppress suspicious graph connections and improve model resilience under adversarial structural attacks.

Graph AI for Public Health

Predictive modeling of diabetes, obesity, and mental-health indicators using socioeconomic, geographic, and graph-based data.

Socioeconomic Prediction with GNNs

Graph neural network and hybrid machine-learning approaches for predicting socioeconomic outcomes using large-scale U.S. Census and geospatial data.

Census2Vec

Geo-embedded representation learning for socioeconomic predictive modeling, integrating geographic structure with learned vector representations.

Dynamic Graph Analytics

Scalable k-core and k-truss algorithms for large dynamic networks using high-performance computing and parallel processing.

AI-Enabled Cybersecurity Research

Research and student mentoring in phishing detection, machine learning, natural language processing, cloud security, and data-driven cyber defense.

Biomedical AI

Research involving white-blood-cell detection, forensic age estimation, medical-image processing, and MRI-based brain tumor analysis.

Responsible & Emerging AI

Research on bias analysis in language models, prompt-based evaluation, responsible artificial intelligence, multimodal AI, and emerging generative AI systems.
